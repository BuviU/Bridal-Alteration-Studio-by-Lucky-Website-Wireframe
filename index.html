Section 3 · About us (tabbed)

About Bridal Alteration Studio by Lucky

At Bridal Alteration Studio by Lucky, we understand that a wedding gown is more than just a dress—it is a cherished memory in the making. With years of dedicated experience in bridal gown construction, delicate lace restoration, boning adjustments, and custom hand-beading, we provide bespoke alteration services tailored to your exact measurements.

From minimalist contemporary gowns to intricate heirloom dresses, our master tailors work with precision and care to ensure your dress flatters your figure comfortably from the first step down the aisle to the final dance.

Section 4 · Services & offerings (tabbed)

Wedding Dress Alterations & Custom Veils

Bodice & Waist Tailoring

Precision taking-in or letting-out of side seams, boning adjustments, and waistline reshaping for a sculpted fit.

Custom Hemming

Multi-layer organza, silk, tulle, and horsehair hem adjustments designed to let you walk and dance effortlessly.

Bust & Support Fitting

Bust restructuring, cup insertion, and strap shortening to deliver complete confidence without visible lines.

Custom Bustle Creation

Expertly engineered American, French, or Austrian bustles that lift your train seamlessly for the reception.

Restructuring & Modifications

Add illusion lace sleeves, lower a backline, convert zip closures into lace-up corsets, or integrate heirloom fabrics.

Bespoke Accessories (Custom Veils & Gloves)

Complete your bridal look with custom-designed accessories created right in our Melbourne studio. We handcraft bespoke single or multi-tier veils with custom length options (cathedral, chapel, fingertip), lace trims, pearl embellishments, and matching bridal gloves.

Final Steam & Press

Professional bridal pressing to ensure every layer of your gown is wrinkle-free for photos.

Overseas Travel Boxing

Express Photo-Shoot Fixes

Rapid turnaround alterations for last-minute photo shoots or tight wedding dates.

The 4-Step Fitting Journey

01

Book Consultation
